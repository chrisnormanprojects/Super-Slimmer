Data & important notes

Packaged foods: live product, barcode and nutrition information comes from Open Food Facts, an open collaborative database. It does not provide Slimming World values.

Plan classifications: the built-in starter library uses public Slimming World guidance for broad Free Food and Healthy Extra categories. Exact branded products are not guessed from nutrition data.

Loose/generic foods: nutrition information can be expanded using the UK Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset (CoFID).

Official plan information: Slimming World states that only Slimming World can designate official values and restricts its estimator to members’ personal use. This app therefore does not scrape, copy or bypass the members-only database.

Not affiliated: Super Slimmer is an independent personal reference tool and is not associated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Slimming World.
