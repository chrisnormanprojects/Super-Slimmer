Data & important notes

Packaged foods: live product, barcode and nutrition information comes from Open Food Facts. It does not provide Slimming World values.

Current supermarket layer: public 2026 supermarket lists include an expanded Aldi-first catalogue plus Lidl, Tesco, ASDA, Morrisons and Sainsbury’s. These are secondary references and should be rechecked if packaging or recipes change.

Plan classifications: the built-in library also uses public Slimming World guidance and current independent references for broad Free Food, Speed and Healthy Extra information.

Not affiliated: Super Slimmer is an independent personal reference tool and is not associated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Slimming World.
